Georgian gastronomy is a symphony of flavours, rooted in the country's long history and culture. Every region has its own specialities, but a few dishes are loved all over Georgia.

## Khachapuri and Khinkali

**Khachapuri** is Georgia's famous cheese-filled bread, and every region bakes its own version, from the round Imeretian khachapuri to the boat-shaped Adjarian khachapuri topped with egg and butter.

**Khinkali** are juicy dumplings filled with spiced meat and broth. Making them is a craft: the dough is pleated by hand to create the perfect balance of texture and taste.

## Grilled Meats and Sauces

Grilled meat, especially **mtsvadi** (shashlik), is a favourite at any gathering. The meat is marinated with herbs and spices and served with sauces such as sour plum **tkemali** and rich walnut-based sauces.

**Khmeli suneli**, a spice blend with coriander and blue fenugreek, gives many Georgian dishes their distinctive depth.

## Wine at the Table

No Georgian meal is complete without wine. Georgian winemaking has roots that go back thousands of years, and the traditional qvevri method produces unique, characterful wines.

## A Celebration of Sharing

Above all, Georgian gastronomy is a celebration of communal dining. Meals are shared with warmth and hospitality at the **supra**, the traditional feast, where a tamada (toastmaster) leads the toasts. A Georgian table is a sensory journey and one of the best ways to understand the country's culture and traditions, passed down through generations.
