itinerary:
  - day: "1"
    title: Arrival in Tbilisi
    text: >-
      Meet your driver at Tbilisi International Airport and transfer to your
      hotel. The rest of the day is free.


      Overnight in Tbilisi.
  - day: "2"
    title: Mtskheta & the Georgian Military Road to Kazbegi
    text: >-
      Morning stop at Jvari Monastery (6th century, UNESCO) overlooking the
      meeting of two rivers, and Svetitskhoveli Cathedral in Mtskheta.


      Drive north along the Georgian Military Road past the turquoise Zhinvali Reservoir and Ananuri Fortress, with a panorama stop at the Friendship Monument above Gudauri.


      In Stepantsminda, ride a 4×4 up to Gergeti Trinity Church (14th century, 2,170 m) beneath Mount Kazbek. Lunch with khinkali in a mountain restaurant. Return to Tbilisi. Overnight in Tbilisi.
  - day: "3"
    title: Kakheti wine country & Old Tbilisi by night
    text: >-
      Drive east to Kakheti, Georgia's main wine region. Visit Bodbe Monastery,
      resting place of St. Nino, and walk the walls of Sighnaghi, the “City of
      Love”, above the Alazani Valley. Tasting of qvevri wines and chacha at a
      family winery, with a homemade lunch.


      Back in Tbilisi, evening walk through the old town: the sulphur baths, the cable car to Narikala Fortress and the city lights from above. Farewell dinner with polyphonic singing and folk dance. Overnight in Tbilisi.
  - day: "4"
    title: Departure
    text: Breakfast, check-out and private transfer to Tbilisi International Airport.
